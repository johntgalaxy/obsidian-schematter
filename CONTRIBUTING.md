# Contributing

This is a personal project shared publicly for others to use and learn from.

**I do not actively maintain this repository for external contributors.** I am unlikely to review or merge pull requests, and I may not respond to issues. This is not a reflection of the quality of any contribution — it is simply a matter of time and priorities.

If you find this useful and want to customize it for your own needs, you are encouraged to fork the repository and make it your own. The MIT license gives you full freedom to do so.

## Bug reports

You are welcome to open an issue, but I cannot guarantee a response or a fix. If you have identified the problem and a solution, a fork is the most reliable path forward.
